Processes equine audiogram into a frequency-weighting curve and applies that curve to long-term acoustic monitoring data.

The primary outputs are:

H-weighted spectrograms showing frequency-specific equine-weighted acoustic energy,
H-weighted broadband time series for comparing acoustic exposure over time, and
unweighted vs. equine-weighted comparisons across recording sessions

At its current stage, the H-weighted values should be interpreted as a relative, horse-specific acoustic weighting index, rather than a calibrated measure of perceived sound level.
